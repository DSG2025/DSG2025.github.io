Tan Son Nhat Airport Pickup to Your Ho Chi Minh City Hotel

Landing in a new city is easier when you already know who is waiting for you. OG Saigon offers private airport transfer from Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) to hotels and accommodation in Ho Chi Minh City, with direct WhatsApp contact before you arrive.

Send us your flight number, arrival date, hotel and luggage details. We’ll use your flight information to follow your arrival and coordinate the pickup, then send clear meeting instructions before you land. You do not need to guess exactly how long immigration, customs or baggage collection will take. From your first message to your hotel drop-off, you deal directly with OG Saigon — simple, personal and easy to follow.

Flight Delayed? No Need to Guess Your Exit Time

International arrivals do not always leave the terminal on schedule. Flight delays, immigration, customs and baggage collection can all change your actual meeting time. Send us your flight details and keep WhatsApp available when you land. We coordinate the pickup around your arrival rather than asking you to predict exactly when you will walk outside.

What We Need From You

  • Arrival date and flight number
  • Hotel or destination in Ho Chi Minh City
  • Number of travelers
  • Number of large bags
  • A WhatsApp contact we can reach around arrival

Private Vehicle for Travelers and Luggage

Our transport service is designed for travelers who want a straightforward private ride instead of arranging a taxi after landing. Vehicle type may vary depending on availability, group size and luggage. Tell us your group size and bag count before booking so we can confirm a suitable private vehicle for your transfer.

Frequently Asked Questions About Ho Chi Minh City Airport Transfers

How will I find you at Tan Son Nhat Airport?

We’ll send you clear meeting instructions before your arrival. Keep WhatsApp available after landing so we can coordinate directly if anything changes.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Send us your flight number when booking. We use your flight information to follow your arrival and coordinate the pickup around the updated timing.

What if immigration, customs or baggage takes longer than expected?

You do not need to predict the exact time you will exit the terminal. Keep us updated on WhatsApp when possible and we’ll coordinate around your actual arrival.

What information do you need for an airport transfer quote?

Can you take us directly to our hotel in Ho Chi Minh City?
