Optional immigration assistance

Standard Airport Fast Track at SGN

Less time in the immigration queue, more time in Saigon. When several flights arrive close together, regular immigration queues at Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) can stretch to one or two hours. Standard Fast Track helps you avoid the general queue by guiding you to a designated priority lane. Our airport partner estimates that immigration with the Standard service usually takes around 15–30 minutes, depending on conditions.

Regular immigration queue at busy times 1–2 hours Reported by some travelers, not a typical or guaranteed wait
Standard Fast Track at SGN 15–30 min Usual estimate from our airport partner, not guaranteed

Actual wait times vary with flight arrivals, staffing and airport procedures. Fast Track does not skip passport checks, and the priority lane may still have a queue. Airport pickup is a separate service.

Arriving in Saigon

Arrival Fast Track

  1. Before flying, receive the confirmed meeting instructions from OG Saigon.
  2. After landing, find the partner's fixed Love Vietnam meeting sign near the agreed immigration-side area.
  3. Airport staff direct you to the designated Standard Fast Track immigration lane.
  4. Complete passport control yourself, collect your luggage and follow your separately arranged driver meeting plan, if booked.
Leaving Saigon

Departure Fast Track

  1. Check in with your airline and drop checked luggage yourself.
  2. Meet the airport assistance team at the location confirmed before departure.
  3. Staff direct you to the designated priority departure immigration lane.
  4. Complete the remaining airport procedures independently. Priority security screening is not included in Standard Fast Track.
Important to know

The airport team guides you to the lane but does not go through passport control with you. Priority lanes may still have queues, and immigration processing times cannot be guaranteed. Meeting points and lane arrangements can change with airport conditions.

To enquire, share arrival or departure, date, flight number and number of travelers. After checking availability, we confirm any passenger name, nationality and lead-contact details needed by the partner. Please do not send passport images in the initial enquiry.

Good to know

Airport pickup & Fast Track questions

What does Standard Airport Fast Track include?

Our airport partner meets you at a confirmed location and guides you to a designated priority immigration lane for arrival or departure. The service is subject to confirmation for your flight, and priority lanes may still have queues.

Will airport staff go through immigration with me?

No. With Standard Fast Track, staff guide you to the lane, but you complete passport control yourself. No clearance time is guaranteed.

Where will I meet the Fast Track staff?

At SGN, the partner uses a fixed “Love Vietnam” sign near the agreed immigration-side meeting area for arrivals. We send specific instructions after confirmation. Your car driver meets you separately after baggage collection.

Can I book Fast Track and a private airport pickup together?

Yes, you can enquire about both in one WhatsApp message. They are separate services with separate meeting points, subject to availability and confirmation.

Does Departure Fast Track include check-in or security screening?

No. You complete airline check-in and checked baggage drop-off yourself. Standard Fast Track is guidance to the designated priority departure immigration lane, not priority security screening.

How do I find my driver at Tan Son Nhat Airport?

We send your meeting instructions before arrival. Keep WhatsApp available when you land so we can coordinate directly if needed.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when booking. We use flight information and direct communication to coordinate around updated arrival timing. Any waiting arrangements are confirmed in advance.

What if immigration or baggage takes longer?

You do not need to predict your exact terminal exit time. Follow the confirmed meeting instructions and update us on WhatsApp when possible.

What information do you need for a quote?

Arrival date, flight number, hotel or destination, group size, number of large bags and a WhatsApp contact.

Is the pickup private and can you take us to our hotel?

Our airport pickup service is arranged as private transport to the confirmed destination. We confirm the vehicle and pickup details before you book.

Which vehicle will we get?

Vehicle type depends on availability, travelers and luggage. Send your details so we can confirm a suitable vehicle for your group.

Can you also arrange intercity transport?

Selected routes beyond Saigon may be available. Share the pickup point, destination, date, number of travelers and luggage for a separate quote.

Can you help us plan a private experience after arrival?

Yes. You can explore our Saigon experiences or request a customized day. It is optional and separate from your airport pickup.
