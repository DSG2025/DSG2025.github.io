Before you land

A calmer arrival starts with a clear plan

International arrivals can take longer than expected. We coordinate using your flight details and the meeting plan rather than asking you to guess exactly when you will leave the terminal.

What we need from you

  • Arrival date and flight number
  • Hotel or destination in Saigon
  • Number of travelers
  • Number of large bags
  • WhatsApp contact around arrival

These details help us confirm the right vehicle and pickup arrangements for your group.

What happens if you are delayed?

Flight delays, immigration, customs and baggage collection can all affect when you reach the meeting point.

Keep WhatsApp available after landing and contact us if your plans change. We will coordinate according to the arrangements confirmed for your booking.

Waiting arrangements and any additional charges, if relevant, are confirmed in your quote. No fixed waiting allowance is assumed.

Good to know

Airport pickup questions, answered

How do I find my driver at Tan Son Nhat Airport?

We send your meeting instructions before arrival. Keep WhatsApp available when you land so we can coordinate directly if needed.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when booking. We use flight information and direct communication to coordinate around updated arrival timing. Any waiting arrangements are confirmed in advance.

What if immigration or baggage takes longer?

You do not need to predict your exact terminal exit time. Follow the confirmed meeting instructions and update us on WhatsApp when possible.

What information do you need for a quote?

Arrival date, flight number, hotel or destination, group size, number of large bags and a WhatsApp contact.

Is the pickup private and can you take us to our hotel?

Our airport pickup service is arranged as private transport to the confirmed destination. We confirm the vehicle and pickup details before you book.

Which vehicle will we get?

Vehicle type depends on availability, travelers and luggage. Send your details so we can confirm a suitable vehicle for your group.

Can you also arrange intercity transport?

Selected routes beyond Saigon may be available. Share the pickup point, destination, date, number of travelers and luggage for a separate quote.

Can you help us plan a private experience after arrival?

Yes. You can explore our Saigon experiences or request a customized day. It is optional and separate from your airport pickup.
