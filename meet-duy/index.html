Founder & lead local host

Meet Duy, founder of OG Saigon.

I’m a Saigonese host and licensed international tour guide. I founded OG Saigon to build a local brand around the way I like to host: personal, curious, reliable and never rushed.

Saigon local International guide license History & local life
My hosting style

Conversation first.

The best tours I have hosted are rarely the ones where I talked the most. They are the ones where guests asked good questions, shared what they were curious about and left with a clearer picture of Vietnam than when they arrived.

I especially enjoy guiding history-focused days at Cu Chi and the War Remnants Museum, but I also love showing the everyday Saigon behind the famous landmarks: food stalls, neighborhood alleys, coffee and the way people actually live here.

What you can expect from me

  • A relaxed pace and straightforward communication
  • Historical context without turning the day into a classroom
  • Personal and local perspectives when they help explain the story
  • Space for different opinions and questions
  • Practical help with transport, food and planning the rest of your stay
