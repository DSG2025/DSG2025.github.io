MEET THEOG

I am Duy. Born and raised in Quận Tư, District 4, with five generations of Saigon roots.

I've spent nine years hosting travelers, but some of my most important lessons about people came long before I became a guide.

01 Raised in Quận Tư

A little streetwise.
A little old-school.

Growing up in old Quận Tư, I saw two very different sides of life.

Outside our door were narrow alleys, street vendors making a living day by day, and people doing whatever they could to get by. Some struggled with debt and informal lenders. Loan sharks and gambling were part of the neighborhood's rougher side.

But there was another side too. People were warm, generous, full of humor, and always ready for a conversation.

At home, education and respect mattered. My family taught us how to carry ourselves and treat others properly.

Growing up between those worlds taught me to listen, read a room, talk to people from all walks of life, and look beyond first impressions.

That's still a big part of who I am.

02 How I host

Nine years,
still curious.

After nine years of hosting travelers, what I enjoy most is still a good conversation and the chance to share a story.

I love history, but I'm just as interested in the people behind it.

No rehearsed speeches. Just good conversations, local stories, and a little street wisdom along the way.

That's the kind of host I try to be.
