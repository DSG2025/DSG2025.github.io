01Our roots / Gốc Quận Tư

Five generations in Saigon.
Raised in Quận Tư.

Before OG Saigon, there was Quận Tư.

I grew up among narrow hẻm, street vendors, morning coffee, and the rougher side of old District 4. At home, education and how we treated others mattered. Living between those two worlds taught me to listen, look beyond appearances, and appreciate the contradictions that make Saigon what it is.

After nine years of hosting travelers, I knew I didn't want to simply rush people between attractions. I wanted to share the Saigon I know, with its history, humor, everyday life, and people.

That's why I started OG Saigon.

02Our approach / Cách mình đón khách

Small enough to stay personal.

We plan the route around your group. Then we leave room for the questions that come up along the way.

History with context, food as part of local life, genuine conversations, and the freedom to follow something interesting down a neighborhood alley.

No scripts. Just real people sharing the city they know.

OG Saigon began with one local host. As we grow, I hope to bring together a small circle of trusted Saigonese, each with their own neighborhoods, memories, and perspectives to share.

03Our longer view / Ngày mai

From a love for Saigon to a way of life.

Hosting travelers is where we begin, but I hope OG Saigon grows into something broader.

A way of sharing how we eat, enjoy everyday moments, take care of ourselves, approach our work, and give something back.

Quận Tư isn't just where I grew up. It's a community of people and stories that shaped who I am.

I hope OG Saigon can eventually contribute something meaningful to that community through genuine neighborhood connections and small local initiatives.

It's a long-term vision. For now, we're starting with what we know best: sharing Saigon, one personal experience at a time.
