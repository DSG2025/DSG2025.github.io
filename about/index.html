Why OG Saigon exists

We don't want to show you everything. We want to help you understand it

OG Saigon began with my love for Saigon, the city my family has called home for five generations. I wanted guests to remember its people, stories and small moments, not just a checklist of attractions.

Our approach

Small enough to stay personal

We plan the route around your group. Then we leave room for the questions that come up along the way.

That might mean spending extra time on a question at Cu Chi, walking into a neighborhood alley because something catches your eye, or changing the order of a private day because the weather and traffic make another route smarter.

  • Local storytelling instead of generic scripts
  • History with context rather than one-line facts
  • Food as part of local life, not just a tasting list
  • Private experiences shaped around your group
  • Clear WhatsApp communication before the experience

Our longer view

From a love for Saigon to a way of life

I started OG Saigon because I love my hometown. Hosting travelers is where we begin, but my hope is that OG Saigon will grow into something broader: a way of life shaped by the choices we make and the people around us.

I think about how we eat, enjoy everyday moments, take care of our health, approach our work and give back to our community.

This is a long-term vision, not a list of services we already offer. For now, we begin with personal experiences and genuine neighborhood connections, one step at a time.

What matters to us

  • Eat well. Appreciate Saigon's food culture and the conversations shared around a table.
  • Live fully. Make time for the little things, from morning coffee to laughter with neighbors.
  • Care for ourselves. Look after our physical and mental well-being, even when life gets busy.
  • Work with purpose. Take pride in honest work, keep learning and treat people with care.
  • Give back. Stay connected to our neighborhoods and find meaningful ways to contribute.
