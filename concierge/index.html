Good to know

Local concierge questions

Do you help business travelers as well as tourists?

Yes. Ask about transport, local orientation and practical arrangements around your meetings or leisure plans. We confirm the scope individually.

Can I ask for local assistance without booking a tour?

Yes. Tell us what you need, your dates and your location. We will discuss whether we can help and provide a quote.

Can I hire an English-speaking local assistant for a day?

Ask about personal local accompaniment for part of a day or longer. Availability, tasks and working hours are agreed in advance.

How much does local concierge support cost?

Each request is quoted individually based on the agreed tasks, time and any third-party costs. Contact us on WhatsApp for a quote before committing.

Can you arrange professional interpretation or supplier verification?

Our focus is practical local assistance and coordination, not professional interpretation, supplier verification or business advisory. Tell us what you need so we can clarify the scope.
