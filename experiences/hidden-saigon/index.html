The Saigon we know

Good food has never been about fancy chairs.

Saigon has a place to eat for every mood. After work, students and neighbors squeeze around small sidewalk tables for a few familiar dishes. On another night, a family or a seafood lover might choose a simple local restaurant for bigger, carefully selected shellfish and a meal worth lingering over.

Both tables belong to the same city. Neither needs a white tablecloth to make the food good.

OG Saigon was founded by a fifth-generation Saigonese with roots in District 4. We share its food streets, lived-in alleys and everyday stories through the eyes of people who know the neighborhood as home.

02 / For those in the know

Good Seafood, Plastic Chairs

Better seafood, not fancier restaurants. Bigger, carefully chosen ingredients at a humble local spot, with plastic chairs and plenty of character.

A Southern start

Special bánh xèo (crispy pancake) · seafood bánh khọt (mini savory pancakes) · drinks at the pancake stop.

The seafood table

  • Larger, selected scallops grilled with scallion oil
  • Selected, high-quality clams steamed with lemongrass (nghêu hấp sả)
  • Golden-style sea snails (ốc hương) with bánh mì for dipping
  • Blue swimmer crab (ghẹ) in tamarind sauce
  • Drinks at the seafood eatery

The stories between bites

District 4 is more than its old reputation.

Its former underworld reputation is part of the story, but it is not the whole neighborhood. District 4 is also home to working families, small businesses, shared alleyways and people who have looked after their streets for generations.

As we walk, we talk about how these streets have changed and why food is such a natural way to get to know the people who live here.

Before you join us

A few useful things to know

Both experiences focus on real local eateries and District 4 neighborhood stories, not luxury dining or a race through food stops.

The rooftop arrangement is different for each option: it is optional and self-paid for Street Food & Local Life, while Good Seafood, Plastic Chairs includes one bottled beer at the rooftop.

Tell us about food allergies or dietary restrictions before booking. Seafood, shellfish and other common allergens feature in the menus, and not every dish can be adapted safely.
