What we'll enjoy

First, a Southern favorite

  • Special bánh xèo (crispy Vietnamese pancake)
  • Seafood bánh khọt (mini savory pancakes)
  • A drink at the pancake stop

Then, the seafood table

  • Selected large scallops grilled with scallion oil
  • High-quality clams steamed with lemongrass (nghêu hấp sả)
  • Golden-style ốc hương (sea snails) with bánh mì for dipping
  • Ghẹ (blue swimmer crab) in tamarind sauce
  • A drink at the seafood stop

Something sweet

Chè, a traditional Vietnamese dessert.

More than what is on the menu

A neighborhood you get to know, not just eat through.

District 4 has its underworld stories, but also generations of families, markets and close-knit alley life. We walk the streets between food stops and share the neighborhood from a local point of view.

OG Saigon was founded by a fifth-generation Saigonese with roots here. For us, the best way to know Saigon is to sit down, eat well and listen.

Before you join us

A few things to know.

This is a casual local seafood evening, not a luxury or fine-dining experience. Simple tables and plastic chairs are part of the atmosphere.

The menu includes drinks at the food stops and one bottled beer at the rooftop. Additional rooftop drinks are self-paid; you pay the difference for a higher-priced choice.

Please share any allergies in advance, particularly seafood or shellfish allergies. Our first-day Saigon guide may also help you get acquainted with the city.
