Ben Duoc Cu Chi Tunnels & War Remnants Museum

Connect the landscape of Ben Duoc with photographs and artifacts at the War Remnants Museum. Plan around 8 to 8.5 hours including travel, with time for historical context and questions.

Why this experience

More context, less rushing

Ben Duoc shows the wartime tunnel network in its landscape, while the museum adds photographs, artifacts and documentary perspectives. Together they invite a wider conversation about the war.

Good to know

Ben Duoc is OG Saigon's standard Cu Chi site for new bookings, generally less touristy than Ben Dinh. The tunnel crawl is optional. The Memorial Temple is a flexible stop rather than a guarantee.

How long does the combined tour take?

Allow approximately 8 to 8.5 hours including travel. Timing and visiting order vary with conditions.
