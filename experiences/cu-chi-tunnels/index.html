Frequently asked questions

Everything you need before you go.

How long does the tour take?

Allow about 6 to 7 hours for a private round trip from central Saigon. We confirm your pickup time and estimated return before booking. Traffic and extra stops may affect timing.

Are entrance tickets and transport included?

Yes. Every private Cu Chi tour booked directly with OG Saigon includes entrance tickets, a private round-trip vehicle, and pickup/drop-off at hotels in central Saigon. Any arrangements outside the central area are confirmed in the quote.

Which Cu Chi Tunnels site will we visit?

Our usual Cu Chi visit is at Ben Dinh. If you prefer Ben Duoc, tell us when you enquire and we will check the arrangements for a private visit.

Do we have to crawl through the tunnels?

No. Entering an underground tunnel is optional. There are outdoor paths and uneven areas, so please mention mobility or health concerns in your first message.

Is it a private tour?

Yes. The direct Cu Chi visit shown here is private for your own group. Tours booked on third-party platforms follow the group format and inclusions in the selected listing.
