Cu Chi Tunnels

Cu Chi Through Saigonese Eyes

See the tunnels as more than a photo stop — understand how the underground network worked, what daily survival looked like and why the area mattered.

Local English-speaking host Private / small-group style Hotel pickup options
Why this experience

More context, less rushing.

The tunnels are fascinating on their own, but the experience becomes much stronger when the engineering, daily life and wartime context are connected into one story.

A typical flow

1
Pickup & the road to Cu Chi

Meet at your hotel or an agreed pickup point and use the drive to set the historical context.

2
Explore the site with a local host

Walk through key sections, hidden entrances, bunkers and tunnel areas with time for questions.

3
Try the underground experience

Crawl through an accessible section if you want — participation is always optional.

4
Traditional wartime food

Try tapioca and tea while learning why simple foods mattered during difficult periods.

5
Return to Saigon

Relax on the ride back and use the time to continue the conversation.

What we focus on

  • Historical context without a rushed lecture
  • Local stories and practical explanations
  • Time to ask questions and choose how much of the tunnels you want to try
  • Small-group or private experience depending on your booking
  • Comfortable transport options
