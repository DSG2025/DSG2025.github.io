Why this experience

Why Visit the Cu Chi Tunnels with a Local Guide?

The Cu Chi Tunnels are more than a famous stop outside Ho Chi Minh City. They are a window into how people lived, moved and survived underground during the Vietnam War. On this Cu Chi Tunnels tour from Ho Chi Minh City, we slow the experience down and connect the tunnels, bunkers, hidden entrances and wartime food into one clear story.

Travel with a local English-speaking host, ask as many questions as you like, and explore at a pace that feels comfortable for you. Crawling through a tunnel section is completely optional. Direct bookings with OG Saigon are arranged as private tours, with hotel pickup options from Saigon.

Cu Chi FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About the Cu Chi Tunnels

Is the Cu Chi Tunnels tour private?

Yes. Direct bookings with OG Saigon are private. If you book through an OTA, the tour format follows the specific product listing you selected, so please check that listing for its group format.

Do I have to crawl through the tunnels?

No. Going inside a tunnel section is completely optional. You can still explore the site, entrances, bunkers and exhibits without crawling underground.

Can you pick us up from our hotel in Ho Chi Minh City?

Hotel pickup can be arranged for many bookings. Send us your hotel name or area when you contact us and we’ll confirm the pickup plan.

What should I wear to the Cu Chi Tunnels?

Light, comfortable clothing and stable walking shoes are a good choice. Parts of the site are outdoors and the ground can be uneven, especially after rain.

Is the tour suitable if I have limited mobility or do not want to crawl?

The underground crawl is optional and the experience can be taken at a relaxed pace. If anyone in your group has mobility concerns, tell us before booking so we can help you understand what to expect.

Can I combine Cu Chi with the War Remnants Museum?

Yes. If you want a deeper historical day, see our Cu Chi Tunnels & War Remnants Museum tour, which connects the tunnel experience with the wider story of the war in Saigon.

Can you customize the Cu Chi itinerary?

Yes. If Cu Chi is only one part of what you want to see, tell us your interests and available time and we can help build a private custom Saigon day around your trip.

How do I check availability?

Send us your preferred date, number of guests and hotel area on WhatsApp. We’ll confirm availability, pickup options and the best format for your group.
