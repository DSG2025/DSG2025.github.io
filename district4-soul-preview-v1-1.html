Good to know

A few practical questions.

Are OG Saigon experiences private?

OG Saigon primarily focuses on private experiences, giving us more flexibility with timing, stops and the pace of your day.

Can you customize an itinerary?

Yes. Send us a list of places, interests or even a rough plan. We’ll help turn it into a realistic itinerary.

Do you provide hotel pickup?

Pickup is available for many experiences. Exact arrangements depend on the experience and your hotel location.

How do I arrange a private experience with OG Saigon?

Send your date, group size and what you are interested in. We’ll confirm availability, help shape the itinerary and provide a quote for your group before you decide.
