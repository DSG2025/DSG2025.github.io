The people behind OG Saigon

Local by design. Founded by Duy.

OG Saigon was founded by Duy, a Saigonese licensed guide, and built around one clear standard: thoughtful local storytelling, relaxed pacing and reliable support from first message to hotel drop-off.

Duy remains the founder and lead host for many experiences, while trusted local drivers and partners help each day run smoothly. The experience should feel personal without depending on one person.
