Good to know

A few practical questions.

Are OG Saigon experiences private?

Direct bookings with OG Saigon are primarily private experiences. This gives us more flexibility with timing, stops and the pace of your day.

Do you provide hotel pickup?

Pickup is available for many experiences. Exact arrangements depend on the experience and your hotel location.

Can you customize an itinerary?

Yes. Send us a list of places, interests or even a rough plan. We’ll help turn it into a realistic itinerary.

How do I book?

Send your date, group size and experience or trip idea. We’ll confirm availability, itinerary, price and payment terms before you commit.

Do I have to crawl through the tunnels at Cu Chi?

No. Going through the tunnels is optional. There is much more to understand and experience at Cu Chi above ground.

Can I contact OG Saigon on WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp is one of the easiest ways to speak directly with us before your trip.
