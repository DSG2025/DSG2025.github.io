SAIGON · CHỢ LỚN · DISTRICT 4
Featured journey · Couple from Poland

One city tour. Then they wanted to see more.

A full-day visit to Saigon’s historic landmarks became lunch and alley walks in District 4, local shopping in Chợ Lớn and much more.

After that first day, the couple requested a night motorbike ride with food and dessert, another outing for shopping and a visit to a longtime tailor connected to our family. They also made plans for a later Cu Chi trip and airport transfer.

City & Chợ LớnNight rideShopping & tailoring
What made it personal

We adjusted stops around what they wanted to explore and buy, helped with fabric and fitting arrangements, and kept different experiences connected without turning the days into a rigid schedule. The later outings were separate requests, not part of the original city tour.

Solo traveler · About 5 hours

Ten kilometers through another side of Saigon.

One guest wanted to see the city on foot. We linked the Nguyễn Thiện Thuật apartments, Hồ Thị Kỷ flower market, Hào Sĩ Phường, Vạn Phật Pagoda and the neighborhoods of District 4, with two Grab rides for the longer stretches.

See the route

We also explored Xóm Chiếu and Tôn Đản, stopped for coffee and returned via landmarks around the Central Post Office, Notre-Dame Cathedral and the former CIA building. It was roughly 10 km of walking with time for stories along the way.

Father & son from the US · 3 days

Three days, from tunnels to river life.

Cu Chi by day, a District 4 motorbike food evening shaped around Dad's tastes, then a full Mekong experience and a city day with Saigon’s historic sites and Chợ Lớn.

See what they did

The Mekong day included a boat ride, traditional music, fruit, honey tea, a coconut candy workshop, sampan rowing, lunch and Vĩnh Tràng Pagoda. We also helped with their airport departure after the tour days.

Couple from Poland · Full day

Mountain views, Cao Đài stories and a good lunch.

An early drive with coffee and bánh mì ready for the road. We visited Bà Đen Mountain, explored the Cao Đài Holy See and made room for local food before heading back to Saigon.

See the local touches

We stopped at a roadside café, explored the mountain's Buddhist landmarks, talked through the history and architecture of Cao Đài, then enjoyed bánh xèo, bánh khọt and Trảng Bàng's signature bánh canh.

Looking for a tailor in Saigon?

A suit, a dress, a fabric you have in mind, or simply somewhere local to start.

On a recent custom trip, we took guests to meet a skilled tailor who has been a friend of our family for years. We helped organize measurements and fabric shopping around the rest of their Saigon plans.

We can help with local introductions, getting around and coordinating a visit. Pricing, fittings, production time and the finished garment are discussed with the tailor and depend on availability and your schedule.
